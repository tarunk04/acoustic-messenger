If devices do not see each other

  • Both devices must use identical radio settings. Open the app on the second device by scanning the QR code above; it carries the settings.
  • Run Self-test on each device. If it fails, the phone cannot hear its own speaker in that band: raise media volume, switch to Near-ultrasonic, or try Audible to confirm everything else works.
  • Use Tone sweep to see which of the 16 tones your hardware passes. Many phone speakers and microphones lose output above about 18 kHz.
  • Disconnect Bluetooth audio. Keep devices 0.3–3 m apart, and keep the microphone uncovered.
  • Acoustic links are half-duplex. The app listens before it talks and retries unacknowledged packets, but a beacon takes about 3–4 s on air.
  • Keep the screen on. Mobile browsers pause audio for background tabs.
