Radio settings

Frequencies are clamped to 14–20 kHz. Changing settings restarts discovery.

Decoder: waiting for a broadcast preamble

A device appears in Nearby only after its beacon preamble, sync sequence and CRC are decoded. Pair starts a targeted four-step request/accept/confirm/complete handshake. A received message is separately CRC-checked. Because acoustic audio is half-duplex in practice, simultaneous transmissions can collide; retry if a packet is missed.
